Greedy Hunter

Eat every orb before your moves run out.

Board size
6 × 6

6 orbs to find

How to play
  • Move one square at a time: tap a square next to the hunter, swipe, or use the arrow keys (or W A S D).
  • Step onto an orb to eat it. Eat them all to win.
  • You have a limited number of moves, shown by the energy bar: plan the shortest route.
  • Bigger boards mean more orbs and more planning.
